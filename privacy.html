Privacy Policy

Grimborn: The Awakening (Android) · last updated 1 November 2026

Grimborn: The Awakening ("the game") is made by ReveoBlade ("we"). This policy explains exactly what the game does with information. It is deliberately short, because the game keeps almost nothing and operates no server that holds player data.

1. Game progress

Your cards, heroes, gear, coins, marks and settings are saved in the app's own storage on your device. They are not uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them. You can also copy a "Save Code" yourself — that text carries your progress to another device, and it only moves when you choose to send it.

2. Facebook sign-in (optional)

The game offers one optional account: Continue with Facebook, built on Meta's official Facebook Login SDK. When you sign in you are shown Facebook's own consent screen and you choose what to approve.

What we receiveYour Facebook user ID, your display name and your profile picture.
WhyTo show your name and photo on your hunter profile card and to keep your account separate from other players.
Where it is keptOn your device only, inside the app's storage. It is never sent to a ReveoBlade server — there isn't one.
What we never ask forYour Facebook password, your email, your friends list, your posts, or permission to post on your behalf. The game requests only the basic public profile.

Signing out in Settings → Facebook Account clears the stored name and photo and ends the Facebook session held by the app. Meta processes your Facebook account under Meta's own privacy policy.

3. Online play and allies

Duels, hunting parties and ally requests travel over a public message relay (broker.emqx.io, with test.mosquitto.org as a fallback) using encrypted WebSockets. Messages contain a room code, a hunter name and the moves or requests taking place at that moment. They are transient: we do not run the relay, we do not log it, and nothing is stored there for later. Anyone who knows a room code can join that room, so share codes only with people you want to play with.

4. What the game does not do

  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or attribution SDKs.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or photo access.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of personal information with anyone.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.

5. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Facebook sign-in is governed by Meta's minimum age for Facebook accounts.

6. Your choices

  • Play without signing in — every mode except the Facebook-linked profile works offline.
  • Sign out at any time in Settings → Facebook Account.
  • Remove everything the game holds — see the Data Deletion Instructions.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the current version always lives at this address.
